# The Abbot - rules summary

Source: Complete Annotated Rules v7.3, pp. 196-198 (plus pp. 203-205, 210-211, 214). In this game the Abbot is part of the base game, as in New Carcassonne (Carcassonne II) (s. 196-197).

## Components
- 5 wooden abbot figures, one per player color (s. 197).
- 8 land tiles with gardens (s. 197). These are tweaks of ordinary base-game tiles (s. 196-197).
- One River I tile in the C II version also has a garden (s. 197).
- Base tile set in C II is otherwise as the original: 72 tiles (+12 River I) (s. 196). One CFCF tile with pennant lost its pennant: 1 with pennant, 2 without (s. 196).
- OKLART: which base tiles carry a garden (and counts per tile type) is shown only as pictures (s. 198).
- Not the same as the old Abbot of the German/Dutch/Belgian Monasteries, which was a normal follower (s. 197).

## Setup
- Each player gets the abbot in their color. All basic rules are otherwise unchanged (s. 197).

## Turn
1. Place a tile by the normal rules (s. 197).
2. Deploy a follower (s. 197-198):
   - When the tile just placed has a cloister or a garden, you may place either a normal follower or your abbot.
   - The abbot must go on the cloister or garden of the tile you just placed.
   - Only an abbot may be placed on a garden (s. 198).
   - The abbot is a specialized follower, analogous to the Mayor in being restricted to certain feature types (s. 203). The figure table (s. 214) lists it as allowed on cloisters but not on roads, cities or farms; it is "presumed" to have limits analogous to the mayor (s. 214).
   - In the turn order the abbot is listed as one of the follower options in the move-wood step, where only one option may be done per turn (s. 205).
3. Taking the abbot back (s. 198):
   - On your turn, in the follower deployment step, if you choose not to place a follower, you may take your abbot back from the board.
   - You then score the abbot's current value, exactly like scoring a cloister at game end.
   - OKLART: footnote 521 (s. 198) says the C II rules speak of "not placing a meeple"; the author suspects it really means doing no other move-wood action at all, but this is not stated.

## Scoring
- Cloister or garden with abbot surrounded by eight tiles: 9 points, as normal cloister scoring (s. 198, 210: "Garden completed 9 points").
- Abbot returned before completion: 1 point per tile (s. 210).
- End of game: an abbot still on the board scores 1 point per tile (the garden/cloister tile plus the surrounding ones) (s. 198, 211). Vineyards add 0 extra points here (s. 211).
- Example (s. 198): returning an abbot whose feature counts 6 tiles scores 6 points.
- Gardens score exactly like cloisters (s. 198).

## Interaction with other expansions
- The abbot counts as a (specialized) follower (s. 203).
- River I in C II includes one garden tile (s. 29, 197).
- OKLART: interaction with other expansions (e.g. effects that forbid deploying a follower) is not specified for the abbot beyond its listing under "deploy a follower" (s. 205).
