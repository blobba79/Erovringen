# The Ferries - Mini #3

Source: Complete Annotated Rules v7.3, s. 147-150, plus s. 29, 189-190, 205-206, 218.

## Components
- 8 wooden ferries (s. 147).
- 8 landscape tiles with lakes (s. 147).
- 1 Crop Circles tile (different from the original Crop Circles expansion tiles) (s. 147, 150). Best played with all 6 crop circle tiles (s. 150).
- Tile distribution: 9 tiles in total, each x1 (s. 150). Exact layouts are image only. OKLART: lake tile layouts (s. 150).

## Setup
- Shuffle the lake tiles into the stack. Keep the ferries in a supply (s. 147).

## Placing a lake tile
- Place the tile as usual. Then the player may place a follower. A follower placed on a road must go on one of the 3 or 4 road segments of the lake tile (s. 147).
- Afterwards the active player must take a ferry from the supply and use it to link two of the road ends on the lake. The ferry joins those ends into one continuous road. A road end on a lake with no ferry is closed (s. 147).
- Ferry placement happens whether or not a follower was placed (s. 147, note 441).
- If the tile with the ferry completes a feature, it scores as usual (s. 147). Roads completed by placing or moving a ferry count in the completion step (s. 206).
- Ferries are neutral and belong to nobody (s. 29).

## Extending a road with a ferry and moving ferries
- When a placed tile extends a road that contains a ferry, the player may (not must) move that ferry (s. 148). The road must actually contain a ferry; a road ending at a lake without a ferry cannot be moved (s. 148, note 442).
- A ferry always connects exactly 2 road ends. It may be moved so it no longer touches the road just extended (s. 148).
- If the extended road has two ferries, only the first one, nearest to the just placed tile, may be moved (s. 148, note 443).
- If roads go out in several directions from the placed tile, the first ferry in each direction can be moved, so up to 4 ferries (s. 148).
- Each ferry may be moved only once per turn. With a new lake tile the new ferry is placed first, then other allowed ferries may be moved (s. 148).

## Order of actions (s. 148)
1. Place tile.
2. Place follower.
3. Place ferry (if a lake tile was placed).
4. Move ferries on other lakes (if the placed tile extended a ferry road).
5. Score features.
- In the full turn order the ferry is placed and moved in Step 5 after the follower step (s. 206). Note: some printed rules wrongly require a follower to be placed on the road first for steps 3 and 4; there is no such requirement (s. 148, notes 444-445).

## Scoring
- No extra scoring; roads score normally. A ferry has no value for majority (s. 218). Ferries cannot be placed on city, farm or cloister and are only on roads that end at lakes (s. 218). They cannot be captured, are no dragon food, cannot use the magic portal (s. 218).
- Followers on roads that become joined or separated by moving a ferry share or lose shared roads accordingly (examples s. 147-148).

## Interaction with other expansions
- Inns & Cathedrals: a ferry lake is not an inn lake, so a road only beside a ferry lake scores 1 point per tile (s. 150).
- Traders & Builders: if a road with a builder is extended only by moving a ferry, no double turn is allowed (s. 150). If moving a ferry puts the builder on a different road than the owner's follower, the builder is returned to its owner, even if the follower is still on a road (s. 150, note 447).
- No rules for The River, Abbot, Princess & Dragon, Mage & Witch or Crop Circles II are given in this section. OKLART: further multi-ferry examples are only pictures (s. 149).
