# The River (River I) - rules summary

Source: Complete Annotated Rules v7.3, pp. 27-30 (plus pp. 267-269 tile list). In this game the River is part of the base game (as in New Carcassonne, which packages River I with the base game, s. 196).

## Components
- 12 new land tiles with river segments plus ordinary features (cities, cloisters, roads, fields) (s. 27).
- Special tiles: 1 spring tile (river source) and 1 lake tile (river mouth); the other 10 are ordinary river tiles (s. 27).
- Original 2001 and 2014 versions have the same river layout and claimable features; they count as the same expansion (s. 28).
- 2014 version (Big Box 5) additionally has 2 tiles with a sheep and 2 tiles with a vineyard (for Hills & Sheep) (s. 28).
- New Carcassonne (C II) version adds one garden (for the Abbot) on one river tile (s. 29; s. 197 confirms one River I tile has a garden).
- Tile list (s. 267-269, River I; images not readable, counts from the text): spring x1, lake x1, city+city+river+river x1, city+river+road+river x1, city+river+city+river x1, farm+farm+river+river x2, farm+river+farm+river x2, farm+river+road+river x1, road+road+river+river x1, road+river+road+river x1. OKLART: the text layout on s. 267-269 mixes River I, River II and GQ11 counts, so the per-type counts above are inferred so that they total 12 (the farm+river+farm+river count of 2 is deduced from the total). Check against the tile images, s. 30 and 267-269.
- OKLART: s. 30 gives distributions for the "Original 2001 version" and "2014 version" only as pictures; the exact tiles per version cannot be read from the text.

## Setup
- Take out the spring and the lake tile. Place the spring in the middle of the table (s. 27).
- Shuffle the other 10 river tiles and make a face-down stack (s. 27).
- The lake tile is not in the stack. New Carcassonne rules place it at the bottom of the stack (s. 27, footnote 40). The older rules place it once the river is finished (s. 27). OKLART: the two formulations differ; either way the lake is the last river tile.
- The field on the spring and lake tiles wraps around the river feature, making one connected farm (s. 27, footnote 39).
- The original starting tile (without river) is either mixed into the river stack or placed as a normal tile as soon as the river is finished (s. 28). OKLART: which of the two to use in this game.

## Turn during the river phase
- Players take turns drawing and placing a river tile as usual until the river stack is empty (s. 27, footnote 41).
- A newly placed tile must continue the river illustration (s. 27, footnote 41).
- Followers may be placed on the river tiles as knights, thieves, monks or farmers (s. 27).
- Followers may not be placed on the river itself (s. 27).
- River segments separate farms (s. 27, footnote 42).
- No immediate U-turn: a 180 degree turn of the river is not allowed (s. 28). Only immediate U-turns are explicitly forbidden, not every later turning back (s. 28, footnote 43). OKLART: whether a straight tile between two turns in the same direction (which can make a later tile unplaceable) should be blocked by the game; the rules only say it can cause problems.
- When the river is finished (lake placed), normal Carcassonne play continues with the remaining tiles (s. 27).

## Scoring
- No river-specific scoring. Cities, roads, cloisters and farms on river tiles score as normal (s. 27).
- Sheep depicted on river tiles (2014 version): only matter with Hills & Sheep. When a Gather the Flock action is done, depicted sheep on those fields are counted and scored with the sheep tokens; they are permanent and can be scored repeatedly (s. 28).
- Vineyards on river tiles (2014 version): count for cloister scoring under the normal vineyard rules (Hills & Sheep) (s. 28).
- Without Hills & Sheep the depicted sheep and vineyards have no function (s. 28).
- Garden on the C II river tile: see abbot.md.

## Interaction with other expansions
- Two River sets combined (River I and II): discard one spring and one lake and make a single river (s. 27, footnote 36).
- Wheel of Fortune: place the Wheel board in the middle first, then the spring so the river flows away from the board. Lay the river and lake by normal rules; afterwards play as normal (s. 28).
- Hills & Sheep: see above (s. 28).
- Abbot: see above and abbot.md (s. 29, 197).

## House rules (optional, s. 29, not official)
- Ban all U-turns; no followers until the whole river is down; move the finished river to the centre; put all river tiles in the bag (any size); mix the plain starting tile into the river; lay the river from lake to spring. Not part of the official rules.
