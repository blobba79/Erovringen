# Mage and Witch (Mini #5)

Source: Complete Annotated Rules v7.3, s. 154-156, plus turn order and scoring pages (s. 204-211).

## Components
- 1 wooden Mage (purple) and 1 wooden Witch (orange) (s. 154).
- 8 land tiles with the mage symbol (s. 154). Big Box distribution: 2+2+1+1+2 = 8 tiles across the Big Box tile lists (brickforteckning).
- The mini also contains 1 crop circle tile; see crop-circles-2.md. Total of the mini: 9 tiles (s. 156).
- Mage and Witch are neutral figures: they belong to nobody once in play (s. 201-202, neutral figures list).

## Setup
- Shuffle the 8 mage tiles into the normal tile stack. Mage and Witch wait beside the scoring track (s. 154, 155).

## Turn
- Normal Carcassonne turn. When a mage-symbol tile is drawn, place it by the usual rules (s. 154).
- In the turn order this is Step 3g: after placing the tile, before placing a follower (s. 154, 205).
- The same Step 3g applies if the placed tile joins the Mage's feature with the Witch's feature (s. 155, 205).
- The active player must place or move exactly one of the two figures (s. 154-155):
  - Neither on the board: bring one into play.
  - Both on the board: move one of them.
  - Only one on the board: choose either to move the one on the board, or bring in the other (s. 154, footnote 455).
  - The player chooses freely between Mage and Witch in all cases (s. 154, fn. 455).
- Target: any segment of an unfinished city or any section of an unfinished road, anywhere on the board (not just on the new tile) (s. 154).
- Mage and Witch may not be on the same road or the same city (s. 154).
- A figure that is moved must go to a different feature than the one it was on (s. 154, fn. 454).
- After this the active player may place a follower as normal (s. 154).
- If no legal target exists (no unfinished city or road): the player must remove the Mage or the Witch from the board if at least one is on it. Otherwise both stay by the scoring track until the next mage tile (s. 155).
- Special case: if the tile placed joins the Witch's feature with the Mage's feature, the active player must move one of them. If the tile also completes a feature, the figure is moved before scoring (s. 155).

## Scoring
- Completed road/city with Mage: +1 point per tile in the feature (s. 155, 210-211).
- Completed road/city with Witch: the feature's points are halved, rounded up (s. 155).
- Road with an Inn or city with a Cathedral and the Witch: first calculate the score using the Inn/Cathedral, then halve it (s. 155, fn. 459).
- Examples: city with 8 tiles and 2 pennants scores 20, Mage adds 8, so 28 for each tied player. Road of 5 tiles with Witch gives 3. Road of 3 tiles with Mage gives 6 (s. 155-156).
- A feature scored with the Mage or Witch on it: the figure is removed from the board (Step 7j) and can return when a new mage tile is played (s. 155, 207).
- Final scoring: Mage and Witch affect roads and cities as during the game (s. 155).
- Unfinished road with Inn or unfinished city with Cathedral, which normally score 0, score 1 point per tile at the end if the Mage is on the feature. Pennants in such a city count 0 (s. 155, fn. 460).
- OKLART: s. 155 does not state what the Witch does to an unfinished feature at the end of the game (presumably normal end scoring is halved; the text only says they affect scoring "as during the game"). Also unclear whether the Witch is removed after final scoring (irrelevant, game over).

## Interaction with other expansions (this game)
- Inns & Cathedrals: see Witch/Mage rules above (s. 155).
- Traders & Builders: Builder rule and the Mage/Witch tile have no stated conflict. OKLART: whether the extra Builder turn (Step 9a repeats Steps 2-8) triggers a second mage move if a mage tile is drawn again; the repeat draws a new tile, so presumably yes (s. 208).
- The Abbot, River, Princess & Dragon, Flying Machines, Ferries, Gold Mines: no interaction stated in the Mage and Witch section.
- Table of what figures can be deployed to (s. 217): Mage and Witch deploy to road and city only, not to farm, cloister, or tower. OKLART: the table text is garbled in extraction; the other columns were not verified.
