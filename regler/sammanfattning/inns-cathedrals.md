# Inns & Cathedrals (Expansion 1) - rules summary

Source: Complete Annotated Rules v7.3, s. 31-34, plus s. 207, 209, 211, 214 (turn order, scoring tables, figure table). First released 2002 (s. 31).

## Components
- 18 new land tiles (s. 31).
- 6 point tiles, "50" on one side and "100" on the other (s. 31). The text does not say how they are used; it is the usual scoring-track marker. OKLART: s. 31 only lists them, no rule text for them.
- 6 big followers, one per player color (s. 31). In the Big Box the sixth set of followers belongs to the base game (s. 31, note 44).

## Tile distribution (s. 34; per-edge grouping from the tile catalogue)
Total 18. Edge order as in the catalogue headings. Counts from the catalogue pages:
- City, city, city, city: 3 (s. 226)
- City, city, city, field: 2 (s. 228)
- City, city, field, road: 1 (s. 234)
- City, city, road, field: 1 (s. 235)
- City, city, road, road: 1 (s. 236)
- City, field, field, field: 1 (s. 241)
- City, field, road, field: 1 (s. 244)
- City, field, road, road: 1 (s. 246)
- City, road, city, road: 2 (s. 248)
- Field, field, road, road: 1 (s. 259)
- Field, road, field, road: 2 (s. 261)
- Field, road, road, road: 1 (s. 263)
- Road, road, road, road: 1 (s. 265)
(Sums to 18.)
OKLART: which of these tiles carry an inn, a cathedral, a cloister, a crossing or a pennant cannot be seen in the text (s. 31, 34, tile pictures). Cathedrals: 2 tiles (s. 33). Inns by a lake: 6 tiles (s. 32). Needs a look at the images.

Special tiles pictured on s. 31:
- One tile has four unconnected city segments (s. 31).
- A tile where a cloister splits the road into two segments (s. 31).
- A tile where a crossing splits the road into two segments (s. 31).
- A tile where thieves may not be placed on the short road stubs between each city and the junction; every junction ends a road (s. 31, note 45). Follow this even with the original tile, where the picture is unclear (s. 31).
- Example tile: the inn lies on the right-hand road segment only, and the field comes to an end there; only BLUE scores for that road (s. 31). OKLART: exact tile identity depends on the picture (s. 31).

## Setup
- Mix the 18 tiles into the draw stack. Each player also gets the big follower of their color (s. 31-32). OKLART: no separate setup text in this section.

## Turn
1. Place a tile as usual. Take care with the special tiles above (s. 31).
2. Deploy a follower: instead of a normal follower you may deploy the big follower (s. 32, s. 207 step 4A "Deploy a follower").
   - It counts as one follower, deployed by the normal rules, and only instead of a normal follower (s. 32, note 46).
   - It can go on road, city, farm and cloister (s. 214).
   - In scoring it counts as two normal followers (s. 32; 2 votes for control, s. 207).
   - It does NOT double points. It only helps win the majority; e.g. alone in a cloister it earns the same as a normal follower (s. 32, note 47).
   - It returns to the owner after scoring like any follower. As a farmer it stays on the farm until the end of the game (s. 32).
3. Score completed roads, cities and cloisters (s. 32-33). Scoring order within a feature: base score, then inn, then cathedral, then other modifiers (s. 207, step h).

## Scoring during the game (s. 32-33, s. 209)
- Completed road with one or more inns: 2 points per tile (instead of 1) (s. 32, 209).
- Completed city with one or more cathedrals: 3 points per tile and 3 points per pennant (instead of 2 and 2) (s. 33, 209).
- An inn only affects the road segment next to it, not every segment on the tile (s. 32, note 50).
- Inn and cathedral tiles may be placed on opponents' roads/cities, including late in the game to strip a big city of points (s. 33, note 51).
- Ransom: if a big follower is captured by a tower (Tower expansion), the ransom is not doubled (s. 32, note 48).

## Scoring at the end of the game (s. 32-33, s. 211)
- Incomplete road with inn: 0 points (s. 32, 211).
- Incomplete city with cathedral: 0 points for tiles and pennants (s. 33, 211).
- Other incomplete features score as usual. A big follower in a farm scores the farm like two farmers.

## Interaction with other expansions in this game
- Mage & Witch: the order of tallying puts inn and cathedral before mage and witch (s. 207). Mage gives +1 point per tile of road/city; witch gives half the feature points rounded up (s. 209). OKLART: whether the witch halves the inn/cathedral-increased score is only implied by the order on s. 207, not stated in the I&C section.
- Traders & Builders, Princess & Dragon, Flying Machines, Ferries, Gold Mines, The River, The Abbot, Crop Circles II: no direct rule in the I&C section.
  - Control of a feature counts the big follower as 2 votes (s. 207); this applies in all combinations.
  - The Abbot: s. 214 table lists the big follower as able to go on road, city, farm and cloister-like features, but not whether it may enter a garden. OKLART: garden placement for the big follower (s. 214).
  - Crop Circles II: the follower type for a crop circle is set by the circle type (s. 208). OKLART: whether the big follower can be chosen or removed this way is not stated.
  - Gold Mines: gold is awarded to the controlling players before points (s. 207, step g); the big follower counts as 2 votes there too.
  - Flying Machines / Ferries: OKLART: no rule in the sections read about big follower use.
- The "spring" tile from another expansion (GQ11) seems to have an inn but no lake, so it is apparently not an I&C inn (s. 32, note 49). OKLART: not clear whether it counts; source says only "seems".
