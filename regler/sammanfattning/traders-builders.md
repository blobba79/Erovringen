# Traders & Builders (Expansion 2)

Source: Complete Annotated Rules v7.3, s. 35-40, plus the turn and scoring pages s. 203-215.

## Components
- 24 new land tiles (s. 35). Tile distribution on s. 40 shows 24 tiles, one of each (x1). The goods symbols on individual tiles are only in pictures: OKLART: which tiles carry wine/grain/cloth symbols and how many of each (s. 40, brickforteckning s. 224-273).
- 6 builders and 6 pigs, one of each per player color (s. 35).
- 20 trade counters: 9 wine, 6 grain, 5 cloth (s. 35).
- The cloth bag is not needed in the digital game (s. 35, footnote).
- Builders and pigs belong to a player but are NOT followers in the current rules (s. 35, 203).

## Setup
- No special setup beyond adding the tiles to the stack and giving each player a builder and a pig (s. 35).

## Tile placement (s. 35)
Placed as usual. Three tiles are special:
- Bridge tile: the bridge is not a crossing. One road runs left-right, the other top-bottom. Four separate field segments.
- Tile with one road ending in a city and the other in a house (not an inn): three separate field segments.
- Tile where a cloister splits the road into three segments.
- A tile with three separate city segments.
- OKLART: the exact tile layouts for these depend on pictures (s. 35).

## Turn: deploying a figure (s. 36-38)
Instead of a normal or big follower the player may deploy the builder or the pig.

### Builder
- Only on the tile just placed, and only on a road or city segment that already contains one of the player's followers (thief, knight, and the like; wagon and mayor also count as supporting followers, s. 214). So the follower is placed in an earlier turn, the road/city is extended in a later turn, then the builder is placed on the new tile (s. 36).
- Other players' thieves, knights or builders on the feature do not stop it. The distance to the follower does not matter (s. 36).
- Road or city only, never a farm (s. 36, 215).
- Builder does not count for majority (s. 37).
- Effect: whenever the owner places a tile that extends the road or city with their builder, they may take a double turn (s. 36). The double turn is optional (s. 36, footnote 58).
- A tile that only completes a feature without extending it does not count. The Abbey tile (Abbot/Mayor expansion, not in this game) completes without extending, so it gives no double turn (s. 36, footnotes 55-56).
- A bridge placed on a tile that extends a road triggers the double turn (s. 36, footnote 57).

### Double turn
- First do the whole first part of the turn: place tile, deploy figure, score. Only then draw the second tile (s. 36, footnote 59, s. 208).
- Second part: draw a tile, place it, optionally deploy a figure, score (s. 36).
- It is really one turn in two identical parts. Things that happen only once per turn (Fairy bonus point, prisoner buy-back, tunnel token placement, flight from plague, plague spread) still happen once (s. 36 footnote 60, s. 39). Placing a tile, resolving special symbols, placing figures and scoring may happen twice (s. 39).
- OKLART: whether removing a knight from a besieged city happens once or twice is unknown (s. 39, footnote 68).
- No chain reaction: extending the builder's feature in the second part does not give a third tile (s. 37).
- If the feature is not completed, the builder stays and gives a double turn on each later extension (s. 37).
- When the feature is completed and scored, the builder and the thief/knight are returned (s. 37).
- A figure may be deployed in both parts. If the first tile completes the feature, the returned builder may be placed on the second tile (s. 37).
- When the player's last thief or knight leaves the feature, the builder returns to the player's supply (s. 37, footnote 62, 65). This also holds if the follower is removed by the dragon, the tower, the princess or a cloister escape (s. 215). Same rule for a wagon or mayor as supporting follower (s. 215).

### Pig
- Only on the tile just placed, and only on a farm that already contains one of the player's farmers. Other players' farmers or pigs may already be there (s. 38).
- Effect: in final scoring, a player with a pig on a farm gets 4 instead of 3 points per completed city on that farm. Only applies if the pig's owner has the majority (or a tie for it) on the farm. Otherwise the pig adds nothing (s. 38).
- The pig does not count for majority, and only farmers decide ownership (s. 38).
- If the owner's last farmer on that farm is removed, the pig is returned. Otherwise it stays until the end of the game (s. 38).
- The pig is never placed on road, city or cloister (s. 215).
- Pigs and builders cannot be captured, seduced or sent to a cloister themselves, but vanish when their supporting follower is removed (s. 215).

## Scoring during the game (s. 38)
- A completed city containing trade symbols is scored as usual. The player who completed it (placed the completing tile) receives one trade counter for each matching symbol in the city (s. 38, s. 206).
- It does not matter whether this player has a knight in the city, or whether any knights are there (s. 38).
- Completing with an Abbey tile also gives the counters to the placing player (s. 38, footnote 66).
- Counters are taken in the scoring step, after the points for the feature (s. 206).

## Final scoring (s. 39)
- Most wine counters: 10 points. Most grain counters: 10 points. Most cloth counters: 10 points. Ties: every tied player gets the full 10 (s. 39, 212).
- Pig: farmers give 4 instead of 3 per completed city with a pig as above (s. 38, 209).
- OKLART: whether a player with 0 counters of a type can win it when nobody has any (not stated, s. 39).

## Interaction with other expansions in this game
- The Abbot (C II, Crop Circles II abbot) is a follower-like figure but is not placed on road or city, so it cannot support a builder (s. 214). OKLART: not stated explicitly beyond the table.
- Inns & Cathedrals: a city with a cathedral or a road with an inn that is incomplete gives 0; no special T&B rule is stated. Footnote 54 only notes that the road end on the T&B tile is a house, not an inn (s. 35).
- Princess & Dragon (old version): the princess tile removes a knight (or builder under old rules); with current rules the builder is not a follower and the builder is removed with its knight. The dragon removing a supporting follower also removes the builder or pig. The Fairy bonus (3 points for a follower next to the fairy at scoring) occurs only once in a double turn (s. 36, 39, 213, 215). Old HiG rules treated builders and pigs as followers; the current rules do not (s. 213).
- Flying Machines, Ferries, Gold Mines, Mage & Witch, Crop Circles II, The River: no T&B-specific rule found in the sections read. Mage and Witch are neutral figures (s. 203). OKLART: whether a builder on a road/city may be extended via a ferry or flying-machine placement is not stated.
- Magic portal rule (not in this game) appears in the rule-set table: the builder or pig may be deployed to unscored features in some rule sets (s. 213).
- House rules (optional, not official): keep drawing while extending the builder's feature, and alternative trade-good scoring (s. 40). Should not be implemented unless specified.
