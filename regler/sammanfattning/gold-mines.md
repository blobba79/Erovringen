# The Goldmines (Gold Mines) - Mini #4

Source: Complete Annotated Rules v7.3, s. 151-153, plus s. 29, 205, 207, 212, 218.

## Components
- 16 wooden gold pieces (s. 151).
- 8 landscape tiles with a gold symbol (s. 151).
- 1 Crop Circles tile (different from the original Crop Circles expansion) (s. 151, 153). Best played with all 6 (s. 153).
- Tile distribution: 9 tiles in total, each x1 (s. 153). Layouts are image only. OKLART: gold tile layouts (s. 153).

## Setup
- Shuffle gold tiles into the stack. Keep the gold pieces in a supply (s. 151).

## Placing gold
- Place the gold tile as usual. Then the player must put one gold piece on that tile and one on an adjacent tile; diagonal neighbours count (s. 151, 205). So 2 gold pieces per gold tile.
- A tile may receive more gold even if it already holds gold (s. 151).
- Gold is never tied to one feature; it applies to all features on its tile (s. 151).
- After gold placement the player may still place a follower normally (s. 151).
- Gold is neutral when in play and belongs to nobody (s. 29), and it has no value for majority (s. 218).

## Order of a turn (s. 152)
1. Place (gold) tile.
2. Place gold pieces.
3. Place follower.
4. Score.
5. Award gold pieces.
- In the full turn order the gold is placed right after the tile in Step 3, and awarded as part of resolving completed features (s. 205, 207).

## Awarding gold during the game
- When a feature is completed, the player with the majority in it receives all gold pieces lying on the tiles of that feature (s. 151).
- A road or city includes every tile that has a segment of it. A cloister includes its own tile and all 8 adjacent tiles (s. 151).
- Castle (from another expansion): the owner can take gold within the 6 tile vicinity: the 2 castle tiles, the 2 tiles to the left and the 2 to the right. The owner has no right to gold of the feature that triggered the castle unless that gold is also within the vicinity or the owner also holds the majority there (s. 151, note 449).
- If several players tie for majority, or several features with gold complete at once, the gold is shared among the claiming players. Distribution goes clockwise starting with the active player, until all gold is given out (s. 152).
- Note 450: the active player (if entitled) picks first, and may pick any piece they are entitled to. The next entitled player in clockwise order then picks. Because of the order, a player who was entitled may miss out if the piece was already taken (s. 152, note 450).
- OKLART: whether a player takes one piece per round only or one per feature. The text says "until all pieces have been awarded" (s. 152), and note 451 says another rules version gives one gold piece per scored feature, which seems to differ. The Hans im Glück rule is one piece at a time (s. 152, note 451).
- Each player keeps collected gold in front of them until the end (s. 152).
- Farmers get no gold (s. 152).

## End of game scoring
- Gold still lying on tiles at game end is removed and scores nothing (s. 152).
- Collected gold scores per piece, depending on how many the player holds (s. 153, 212):
  - 1-3 pieces: 1 point per piece
  - 4-6 pieces: 2 points per piece
  - 7-9 pieces: 3 points per piece
  - 10 or more: 4 points per piece
- Example: 7 pieces are worth 7 x 3 = 21 points (s. 153).

## Interaction with other expansions
- Castle see above (s. 151). Gold tile symbol and the turn order step: gold is placed before follower deployment (s. 205).
- Gold is not a figure that can be captured, eaten by the dragon, or sent to Carcassonne (s. 218).
- No specific rules for The River, Abbot, Inns & Cathedrals, Traders & Builders, Princess & Dragon, Mage & Witch or Crop Circles II are given. OKLART: how the Abbot or a Cloister-like feature from Abbot counts in the "8 adjacent tiles" rule is not stated (s. 151).
