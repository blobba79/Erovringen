# The Princess and The Dragon (Expansion 3, old version with princess and volcano)

Source: Complete Annotated Rules v7.3, s. 52-62. Dragon movement in detail: see `princess-dragon-dragon.md`.

## Components
- 30 land tiles (s. 52): 6 volcano, 12 dragon, 6 magic portal, 6 princess.
- 1 dragon, 1 fairy (neutral figures) (s. 52, 203).
- Tile distribution page (s. 62) shows only pictures with counts (x1/x2), total 30. OKLART: the exact per-tile layout (which tile has which feature, which one is x2) cannot be read from text (s. 62).
- Every new tile carries one symbol: volcano, dragon, princess or magic portal. The symbol affects the "Deploy a follower" step (s. 52).
- Two special tiles (s. 52):
  - Tunnel tile: road is NOT broken, nor are the farms at top/bottom. If tunnel tokens (The Tunnel) are used, the road on the first tile is broken (s. 52, fn 116).
  - Cloister-in-city tile: when deploying, the player must clearly choose city or cloister. Follower on the cloister scores the cloister at 8 surrounding tiles, even if the city is incomplete. A monk may be placed even if the connected city already has a knight, and vice versa (s. 52).

## Setup
- The dragon and the fairy start at the edge of the playing field, off the board (s. 53, 58).
- Until a volcano has been placed the dragon is not in play. If a dragon tile is drawn before that: set it aside, draw another tile. When the dragon enters play, mix all set-aside dragon tiles back into the supply (s. 58).
- House rule (optional, not core): put dragon tiles back and redraw at once if the dragon is not yet in play (s. 61).

## Turn order changes (s. 205-206, 213)
- Step 1, begin turn: if the fairy is next to one of your followers, score 1 point (s. 204, 54).
- Step 3, place tile: volcano symbol means the dragon is placed on this tile (s. 205). Princess symbol: see princess below.
- Step 4 (deploy): fairy move is an alternative to the one deploy action (s. 206, 53).
- Step 5, resolve: dragon symbol on placed tile means the dragon moves (HiG rule) (s. 206). Dragon moves BEFORE scoring (s. 56, 57 fn 152).
  - OKLART: the RGG edition (stated in fn 139, s. 56, and the table on s. 213) moves the dragon AFTER scoring. The HiG/ZMG rule (before scoring) is the current one, so use it unless decided otherwise.
- Step 7, scoring: fairy next to a follower in a feature being scored gives its owner 3 points (s. 207).

## Volcano (s. 55)
- The player placing a volcano tile must immediately move the dragon to that tile from its current spot.
- On a volcano turn the player may NOT deploy a follower, builder, pig or any other figure to that tile (s. 55, fn 133-134). Phantom is also forbidden (s. 206).
- Allowed on a volcano turn: move the fairy by the usual rules; place a barn if the tile creates a barn site; play a tower piece; place a follower on the Wheel of Fortune; place little buildings; build a bridge (s. 55, fn 134).
- Followers may never share a tile with the dragon (s. 55).
- Fairy may be moved on a volcano turn, since the fairy may be moved whenever no follower is placed (s. 55, fn 135).

## Magic portal (s. 59)
- Player placing a portal tile may deploy a follower either to that tile or to any tile already in play.
- Normal rules still apply: the target feature must be unoccupied and not completed (completed counts whether scored or not) (s. 59, fn 158).
- If the portal tile itself completes a feature (e.g. a cloister), the player cannot send a follower there (s. 59, fn 160).
- Not allowed via portal: City of Carcassonne, Wheel of Fortune (s. 59, fn 157). Allowed: features outside those, and school-tile features outside the school (s. 59, fn 159).
- Builders, pigs and barn cannot use the portal (not followers) (s. 59, fn 153-154). Mayor and wagon can. Phantom can, but the portal counts as a feature: the first follower placed on the portal claims it, so a wooden follower and the Phantom cannot both use it in one turn (s. 59, fn 155).
- The portal is never completed (s. 59, fn 156).

## Princess (s. 60)
- Tile placed by the usual rules. If it extends a city that holds one or more knights, the player MAY remove one knight (normal or big), mayor or wagon, including their own, and return it to its owner.
- Player chooses freely among all players' knights in the city.
- If the last knight of a player is removed from a city where that player has a builder, the builder is also returned (s. 60, fn 167).
- If the player removes a knight, the player may not deploy or move any figure that turn (follower, builder, pig, mayor, barn, wagon, fairy, also not Phantom) (s. 60, 61, fn 168).
- If the player does not remove a knight, deploy as normal (s. 60).
- A follower on a tower is not in the city and cannot be seduced (s. 60, fn 165).
- Version note: older/RGG/ZMG rules forced removal of a knight; the current rule makes it optional (s. 60, fn 161, s. 213). Use "may".
- Princess tile starting a new or empty city: no removal possible, deploy as normal (s. 60, fn 166).

## Fairy (s. 53-55)
- Placement: a player who does not deploy a follower, builder or pig this turn MAY instead move the fairy next to one of his own followers. If already on a tile, it moves to another (s. 53).
  - Also an alternative to a tower piece or any other figure (s. 53, fn 119). Moving the fairy forbids placing any other figure: builder, pig, mayor, barn, wagon (s. 55). Phantom may still be placed afterwards (s. 55, fn 131).
  - Using the Festival to remove a follower counts as an action, so no fairy move (s. 53, fn 118).
  - Fairy can only stand next to a follower, not a builder or pig (s. 53, fn 122). A player with no followers on the board cannot move it (fn 123). Fairy may go to a tile whose only follower is on a tower (fn 124).
  - Only ONE follower is "next to" the fairy. A second follower on the same tile (e.g. Flying Machines) is not next to it and gets no bonus (s. 53, fn 120).
  - Fairy may be moved when a follower cannot be deployed (volcano, out of followers) (s. 55, fn 135).
- Effect 1, protection: the dragon cannot enter the fairy's tile, so figures there are safe (s. 54).
- Effect 2, 1 point at start of own turn if the fairy is next to one of your followers (s. 54). With a builder double turn, this is scored only at the start of the first turn (s. 54, 55).
- Effect 3, 3 points at scoring: if the fairy is next to a follower in a feature being scored, its owner gets 3 points, regardless of majority or any other points (s. 54, fn 128). The follower returns to its owner, the fairy stays (s. 54).
  - If you complete a city and place no follower, you may move the fairy to a follower in the city first and get the 3 points (deploy phase comes before scoring) (s. 54, fn 130).
  - Heretic/monk challenge loser: both followers are returned, so no fairy bonus (s. 54, fn 129).
- Fairy does not protect against capture by the tower (s. 55).
- Fairy cannot be placed in Carcassonne City or similar special places; it stays "not a special figure" (s. 216). OKLART: s. 216 table text is garbled on this point.

## Scoring summary
- Fairy: +1 at turn start; +3 per scored feature it stands beside a follower of yours (s. 54).
- Dragon and princess give no points themselves, they remove figures (s. 56-57, 60).
- Figures removed by dragon/princess return to owners' supplies with no points (s. 57, 60).

## House rules listed (not core) (s. 61)
- Claim the fairy point until the bag is handed on.
- Drop the 1-point bonus; give 5 points for a protected follower completing a feature.
- Fairy does not block the dragon from entering but still protects figures on its tile.
