# Crop Circles II (Mini #7)

Source: Complete Annotated Rules v7.3, s. 164-166, plus turn order (s. 208).

## Components
- 6 land tiles with crop circles (s. 164). No separate set: one tile was packed in each of the 6 earlier minis (s. 166). In the Big Box they appear once in each of six tile lists, 6 in total (brickforteckning).
- Symbols: pitchfork, club, shield (s. 164). Which tile has which symbol: OKLART (only shown in pictures, s. 166).
- Crop fields (the yellow areas) are their own features, separate road and field segments (s. 166, fn. 476). OKLART: how they are drawn on each tile (pictures, s. 166).
- These are different tiles from the original Crop Circles expansion (s. 156, fn. 461), but the rules return to the original Crop Circles style (s. 166).

## Setup
- Shuffle the 6 tiles into the stack (s. 164). Playable with a single tile, but best with all 6 (s. 156).

## Turn
- The active player places the tile as normal and plays a normal turn, including scoring (s. 164). In the turn order this is Step 8e, after scoring (s. 208).
- Then the active player chooses A or B for all players (s. 164-165, fn. 474):
  - A) Each player may (optional) take a follower from supply and place it next to one of their own followers already on a tile.
  - B) Each player must remove one of their own followers from the board back to supply.
- Order: starting with the player to the active player's left, the active player last. Then the next player takes a normal turn (s. 164-165).
- Follower type depends on the symbol (s. 164):
  - Pitchfork: farmers (on farm segments).
  - Club: thieves (on road segments).
  - Shield: knights (on city segments).
- Option A: the new follower must go to a feature that already contains a follower of the same type (farmer next to farmer, thief next to thief, knight next to knight) (s. 165). It need not be the new tile (s. 165, example).
- A player without a follower of the required type on the board is skipped (s. 165). With A, a player with no follower in supply simply cannot deploy (s. 164, fn. 474).
- Any follower in a city counts as a knight, including Mayor or Wagon (s. 165, fn. 475).
- Example B: the active player scored a city and removed his follower, then chose B. Everyone removes a farmer; he has none and removes nothing (s. 165).

## Which figures are affected in this game
This game has no Mayor or Wagon. Affected:
- Ordinary followers (meeples) on a farm (farmer), road (thief), or city (knight).
- Not affected: Abbot (on cloister or garden) and neutral pieces (Mage, Witch and others), since they are not farmers, thieves, or knights (s. 164).
- OKLART: whether the Big Follower (Inns & Cathedrals), Pig and Builder (Traders & Builders) count. The text only says "follower of the same type" and that any follower in a city is a knight (s. 165); Builder, Pig and Big Follower are not mentioned.
- OKLART: whether Flying Machines, Ferries, or Gold Mines change anything; nothing is stated.

## Scoring
- No points are given by the crop circle itself, and no end-of-game effect is stated (s. 164-165).

## Interaction
- With Traders & Builders: Step 9a repeats Steps 2-8 once for a Builder extra turn (s. 208), so Step 8e would apply in each repeat. OKLART: whether the crop circle resolves after the first or after the extra turn if the first tile is a crop circle.
