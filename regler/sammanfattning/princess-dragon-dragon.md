# The Princess and The Dragon: the dragon in detail and interactions

Source: Complete Annotated Rules v7.3, s. 55-58, 203-218. Main file: `princess-dragon.md`.

## When the dragon is placed and moves
- Dragon starts at the edge of the board, off any tile (s. 53, 58).
- Volcano tile placed: the dragon is moved at once onto that tile from wherever it is (s. 55). This is a placement, not a movement phase.
- Dragon tile placed: the placer may first deploy a follower or move the fairy as usual, then (before scoring) the game is interrupted and the dragon moves (s. 56).
  - Tower: placing a tower piece is an alternative to deploying, so prisoners are taken BEFORE the dragon moves (s. 56, fn 138).
  - The fairy may be placed first to protect a follower before the dragon moves (s. 56, fn 137).
  - Movement happens before scoring, so the dragon may clear a city, cloister or road that the dragon tile just completed before it scores (s. 57, fn 152). OKLART: RGG edition scores first, then moves the dragon (s. 56 fn 139, s. 213). HiG/ZMG (before scoring) is the current rule; s. 206 also puts it in Step 5, before Step 6 scoring.
- Dragon tile drawn while the dragon is not yet in play: set aside, draw another; when the dragon enters play, shuffle set-aside dragon tiles back (s. 58).

## Movement procedure (s. 56)
- Starting with the active player and continuing clockwise, each player in turn moves the dragon exactly one tile, horizontally or vertically (no diagonals).
- The dragon makes 6 moves in total, whatever the number of players (so with fewer than 6 players, play goes around again) (s. 56, 58 example).
- The dragon may not enter a tile twice. It may not go back to the tile where it started (s. 56, fn 140). (Here "twice" includes tiles it has already visited this movement.)
- It may not enter the fairy's tile (s. 56, 54).
- It may not enter the City of Carcassonne (s. 57, fn 141, 148).
- Dead end: if the dragon is on a tile from which no legal move exists, its movement ends early (s. 57).
- If the player whose turn it is to move the dragon has no legal move in that direction, the dragon cannot move: the example shows player D unable to move right and choosing another direction (s. 58). OKLART: whether a player may "pass" while a legal move exists for another direction; the example only shows choosing a different legal direction. The rule text says each player must move the dragon.
- When finished, play continues normally (s. 57), i.e. scoring follows.
- Example (s. 58), 4 players: A up, B left, C down, D left (could not go right), then A up, B up = 6 moves; BLUE and RED followers on the entered tiles were returned.

## What the dragon eats (s. 57)
- On entering a tile it removes ALL figures on it, of every player, returning them to owners: normal follower, big follower, pig, builder, follower on a tower, mayor, wagon.
- Eats even a follower in a tower along with others on that tile (s. 57, fn 147).
- Does not remove a follower in a castle, since it is not on a single tile (s. 57, fn 149-150).
- Does not remove the barn (it stands on four tiles) (s. 57, fn 145).
- Neutral figures are not eaten, including Mage and Witch (s. 57, fn 146). Dragon may stand on the same tile as the Mage or Witch (s. 56, fn 143).
- Gold pieces, ferries, flier die: not dragon food (s. 218). Count, fairy, tower blocks: not eaten (s. 216). (Gingerbread Man in the iOS version can be eaten (s. 57, fn 146); not relevant for this game.)
- Removed figures are returned without points (s. 57).
- Followers and dragon never share a tile: a volcano tile cannot be given a follower (s. 55).

## Last follower rule (s. 57, fn 151, s. 213, 215 fn @)
- If the dragon removes a player's last thief, knight or farmer from a road, city or farm, that player's builder (road/city) or pig (farm) in that feature is also removed.
- Builders and pigs are not followers.
- Current rule: removed. Old rule left them in play (s. 213).

## Dragon and the Carcassonne City / Wheel of Fortune (s. 56, fn 141-142)
- The dragon can fly over tiles around Carcassonne City or around the Wheel of Fortune and eat followers on those landscape tiles, but not those inside the City or on the Wheel itself.
- Count is never eaten.

## Interactions with other expansions in this game
- The River: no interaction found in the text read (s. 52-62, 203-218). Nothing to add.
- The Abbot (Carcassonne II): abbot is listed as dragon food: yes, also can be used with the magic portal and fairy friend, cannot be seduced by the princess (s. 214, 203).
- Inns & Cathedrals: big follower is eaten like a normal follower; princess may remove a big follower like a normal one (s. 60, fn 162; s. 214). Big follower counts as 2 for majority (s. 214).
- Traders & Builders: dragon eats builder and pig (s. 57). Builder double turn: fairy 1-point bonus only at the start of the first turn (s. 54, 55). Builders and pigs cannot be moved next to the fairy, cannot use the portal (s. 53, 59), and the princess removes a builder only when the last knight in that city leaves (s. 60, fn 167). Trade-good tokens: no interaction found.
- Flying Machines (flier): fn 120 (s. 53) says a second follower on the same tile (e.g. via Flying Machines) is not "next to" the fairy. Step 4B says the flier and the magic portal may be used if not used in 4A (s. 206). OKLART: nothing found on dragon vs flier landing or flier die vs volcano turn beyond this (s. 206 forbids Phantom after volcano).
- Ferries: ferry is neutral and not eaten by the dragon (s. 218). Followers on ferry roads are eaten like any road follower. Nothing else found.
- Gold Mines: gold pieces are not eaten and stay on tiles (s. 218). Nothing else found. Gold placement happens in Step 3 (s. 205); OKLART whether it is placed on a volcano tile where the dragon stands (no text found).
- Mage & Witch: not eaten, may share a tile with the dragon (s. 56-57). Mage/witch move happens in Step 3 (s. 205); dragon moves in Step 5 (s. 206).
- Crop Circles II: no interaction found in the text read.
- Tower: fairy does not protect against capture (s. 55). Princess cannot seduce a follower on a tower, but the dragon eats it (s. 60, fn 165).
- Phantom: allowed after moving the fairy (s. 55, fn 131); forbidden after volcano (s. 55, fn 134, s. 206); forbidden after princess removal (s. 60, fn 168).
- Mayor/wagon: eaten by the dragon, captured by tower, seduced by princess (s. 57, fn 144; s. 60).
- Festival: using it to remove a figure counts as the deploy action, so no fairy move (s. 53, fn 118).
- Wheel of Fortune / Carcassonne City: portal cannot deploy there (s. 59, fn 157).
