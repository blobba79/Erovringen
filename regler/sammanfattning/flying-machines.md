# The Flier (Flying Machines) - Mini #1

Source: Complete Annotated Rules v7.3, s. 139-142, plus s. 205-206 (turn order).

## Components
- 1 special die with faces 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3 (s. 139). The game rolls it digitally.
- 8 landscape tiles with an aircraft (flying machine) symbol (s. 139).
- 1 Crop Circles tile (s. 139). It is a different tile from the one in the original Crop Circles expansion (s. 141). The mini is playable with a single crop circle tile, but best with all 6 (s. 141).
- Tile distribution: 9 tiles in total, each tile type x1 (s. 142). The text gives no per-tile feature layout; the picture on s. 142 is needed for that. OKLART: exact tile layouts (s. 142, image only).

## Setup
- Shuffle the aircraft tiles into the normal tile stack. Keep the die ready (s. 139).

## Turn
- Play is normal Carcassonne. The aircraft tile is placed by the usual rules (s. 139).
- With the follower action the player may place a follower normally on a road or field (s. 139).
- Alternative: place a follower as a flier on the flying machine symbol of the tile (s. 139). The symbol has an arrow pointing horizontally, vertically or diagonally; this is the direction of flight (s. 139).
- Then the player rolls the die. The result (1-3) is the number of tiles the follower flies in a straight line along the arrow (s. 139).
- The follower must be placed on the tile where the flight ends, if possible, and the active player chooses which feature on it (s. 139). Restrictions (s. 140):
  - Only unfinished road, city or cloister.
  - May be an unoccupied feature or one already occupied, by own or opposing followers (for example two followers on one cloister or one city segment).
  - Never on a field, even if no farmer is there.
  - Never on a spot with no tile.
- If no legal landing exists (only finished features/fields, or no tile there), the follower goes back to the player's supply and the player may place no more followers this turn (s. 140).
- A feature finished by the tile just placed counts as complete at once, so the flier cannot land on it (s. 140).
- In the turn order, the flier is a "follower placement" option in Step 4A; the flier may be used in Step 4B (phantom step) only if it was not used in 4A (s. 206).

## Examples (s. 141)
- Roll 3 to a spot with no tile: the follower is not placed. Roll 2 in the same situation: cloister or city (even if occupied) is allowed, the finished road is not.
- Another example: roll 2, the road is finished and the field is forbidden, so the follower must go in the city, together with another player's follower.

## Scoring
- The flier adds no scoring of its own. The follower scores as a normal follower on the feature it lands on.

## Interaction with other expansions
- Phantom (from another expansion) can be a flier, but if a normal follower already used the flying machine feature, the phantom cannot use it too; it then goes only on a road or field (s. 139, note 421).
- Mayor can be a flier but only lands on an unfinished city. If there is none on the landing tile, it returns to the supply (s. 140, note 425).
- Not allowed to land inside the City of Carcassonne or on the Wheel of Fortune, but may land on features outside them, also school tile roads (s. 140, note 423). Cannot land on a magic portal or anything other than road, city or cloister (s. 140, note 424).
- Of the expansions in this game, only the Mayor-type rule above (Traders/other) is named; no specific rules for The River, Abbot, Inns & Cathedrals, Traders & Builders, Princess & Dragon, Mage & Witch or Crop Circles II are given in this section. OKLART: whether the Abbot (C II) can fly and whether a builder or pig can be a flier (not stated, s. 139-141).
- The flier die is neutral and not deployed anywhere, just rolled (s. 218).
- Tile counts in the tile list: The Flier appears with tile entries x1 each on s. 142 and in the tile index (s. 224-273).
