# Mage & Witch

The mage and the witch are two neutral figures that you place on a road or city. The mage gives bonus points, the witch halves the score.

## What is included
- 1 mage and 1 witch.
- 8 tiles with a mage symbol.
- An extra tile with a crop circle, described under Crop Circles II.

## How a turn works
- When you place a tile with a mage symbol, right after placing it you must place or move exactly one of the two figures.
- If neither is on the board, you bring one into play. If both are on the board, you move one. If one is on the board, you choose freely: move it, or bring in the other.
- The target is an unfinished road or city anywhere on the board. The mage and the witch may not be on the same road or in the same city, and a figure you move must end up on a different feature than before.
- If there is no legal target, you remove a figure that is on the board.
- Then you deploy a follower as usual.

## Scoring
- When a road or city with the mage is scored, you get **1 extra point per tile** in it.
- When a road or city with the witch is scored, the score is **halved**, rounded up.
- An inn on the road or a cathedral in the city is counted first, then halved for the witch.
- After scoring, the figure is removed from the board and can return when the next mage tile is placed.
- At the end of the game the same applies. An unfinished road with an inn or city with a cathedral, which would otherwise score nothing, gives 1 point per tile if the mage is there.

## Good to know
- The mage and the witch are not eaten by the dragon and can share a tile with it.
- They can only stand on roads and cities, never on fields or cloisters.
- The game counts the points for you, and the phone shows the legal targets.
