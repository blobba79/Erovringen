# The Flying Machines

With flying machines you can send a follower through the air to another tile. A die decides how far it flies.

## What is included
- 8 tiles with a flying machine. An arrow on the flying machine shows the direction: straight or diagonal.
- A die with the faces 1, 1, 2, 2, 3 and 3. The game rolls it for you.

## How a turn works
- Place the tile as usual.
- Instead of placing a follower on a road or field you can place it as a **flier** on the flying machine. The die is rolled, and the follower flies that many tiles in the direction of the arrow.
- The follower lands on the tile where the flight ends, and you choose which feature on it. It must be an unfinished road, city or cloister. It may be free or already occupied, by you or by someone else.
- The follower may never land on a field, or on a space with no tile.
- If there is no legal landing, the follower returns to your supply, and you may not place any more followers this turn.
- A feature the new tile has just completed counts as complete, so you cannot land there.

## Scoring
- The flier scores nothing of its own. It counts as an ordinary follower on whatever it lands on.

## Good to know
- You cannot land inside the City of Carcassonne, on the Wheel of Fortune or on a magic portal.
- The phone shows where the flier can land, and the game rolls the die and handles the rest.
