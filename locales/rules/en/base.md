# Base game

Together you build a landscape of tiles with roads, cities, cloisters and fields, and place followers to score points on what is built.

## What is included

- 72 landscape tiles. One of them is the starting tile, which is already on the table when the game begins.
- Each player has eight followers in their colour. At the start all score markers are at zero.
- 2 to 6 players. The tiles are shown on the big screen and you play from your phone.

## How a turn works

Players take turns. Your turn has three steps:

- **Place a tile.** You get a tile, rotate it and place it on the big screen. At least one edge must touch a tile already there, and all edges that meet must match: road to road, city to city, field to field. Touching only at a corner does not count.
- **Place a follower (optional).** You may place one follower on the tile you just placed, and only on that tile. The follower becomes a knight in a city, a thief on a road, a monk in a cloister or a farmer in a field. You may not place a follower if the road, city or farm already has a follower on it, not even your own. This is checked along the whole feature.
- **Scoring.** If something is completed by your tile it is scored immediately and the followers return to their owners. The phone and the game do the counting.

If you hold several tiles (chosen at the start) you pick which one to play and refill your hand after the turn. If none of the tiles in your hand can be placed anywhere, you may swap a tile away.

## Scoring during the game

- **Completed road:** 1 point per tile. A road is complete when both ends finish at a city, a cloister or a crossing, or when it forms a loop.
- **Completed city:** 2 points per tile and 2 extra per pennant. The city must be fully enclosed by walls.
- **Completed cloister:** 9 points when surrounded by eight tiles (1 per tile, the cloister tile included).
- **Several followers:** the player with the most followers on the road or city scores all the points. In a tie, every tied player scores in full. Two followers do not double the points.
- **Farmers** do not return during the game. Fields give no points until the game ends.

## End of the game

The game ends when the last tile has been placed. Anything completed by that tile is scored as usual. Then final scoring takes place:

- **Incomplete roads, cities and cloisters:** 1 point per tile. Pennants in incomplete cities give 1 point each. An incomplete cloister gives 1 point for itself plus 1 per neighbouring tile.
- **Farms:** the player with the most farmers on a farm scores 3 points for each completed city that borders the farm. In a tie, all tied players score the same. Incomplete cities give nothing. The city must have a wall edge facing the farm; touching at a single corner is not enough.

The player with the most points wins.

## Good to know

- A cloister may lie next to other cloisters, and you do not need all eight neighbours to place it.
- You may place a farmer on the field around a cloister. The cloister then stays unoccupied.
- If you complete a city or road you just placed a follower on, it is scored and the follower returns at once, but it cannot be placed again that turn.
- If you have no followers left you simply place tiles.
- Fields, roads and cities can run into each other over many tiles. If you place a tile that joins two farms that already hold farmers, you may not add a new farmer to it.
