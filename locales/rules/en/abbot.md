# Abbot

The abbot is a special piece that can only stand on cloisters and gardens, and that you can take back whenever you like to score points.

## What is included

- One abbot per player, in the player's colour.
- Some tiles have a garden instead of, or in addition to, a cloister.

## How a turn works

- You place your tile as usual. If the tile has a cloister or a garden you may place either an ordinary follower or your abbot. Only one piece per turn.
- The abbot can only stand on the cloister or garden of the tile you just placed. Only the abbot may stand in a garden.
- **Taking the abbot back:** on your turn, instead of placing a piece, you may take your abbot back from the board. You then score the points it is worth at that moment.

## Scoring

- A garden is scored exactly like a cloister. Surrounded by eight tiles it gives 9 points.
- If you take the abbot back before it is complete you score 1 point per tile, the garden or cloister tile and its neighbours included. An abbot on a tile with five neighbours therefore gives 6 points.
- If the abbot is still on the board when the game ends it also scores 1 point per tile.

## Good to know

- The abbot is a kind of follower, but it may only stand on cloisters and gardens, never on roads, cities or fields.
- If the timing suits you, take the abbot back early and use it again on a new tile.
- The game does the counting and shows how much you would get before you decide.
