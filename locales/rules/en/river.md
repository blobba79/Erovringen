# River

The River is a small extra set of tiles that is laid first, before the real landscape begins to grow.

## What is included

- 12 river tiles. One is the spring, one is the lake and ten are ordinary river tiles with roads, cities, cloisters or fields.
- By default two river sets are used: 20 ordinary river tiles, but still only one spring and one lake (a rule variant that can be changed at the start).

## How a turn works

- The river is always laid before any other tiles. The spring goes first, then the players take turns placing river tiles. Each tile must continue the river.
- During the river phase you only hold river tiles. The lake comes last. A player with no river tile is skipped.
- When the lake is placed the river is finished. Then the ordinary starting tile is placed, and the base game is played as usual.
- The river may not turn so that it gets stuck, and may not make an immediate U-turn (180 degrees). The game only shows the positions that are allowed.
- You may not place a follower on the river itself, but you may on the roads, cities, cloisters and fields of the river tiles.

## Scoring

The river gives no points of its own. Roads, cities, cloisters and fields on river tiles are scored as usual. The river does split fields, so fields on different sides of the river become different farms.

## Good to know

- At the start you can choose other river rules: only one river set, mixing the starting tile in among the river tiles instead of placing it after the lake, or allowing dead ends.
- If you hold several tiles you pick among the river tiles. If none can be placed you may swap one away.
- The field on the spring and the lake wraps around the river and forms one connected field.
