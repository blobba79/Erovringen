# The Princess & the Dragon

This expansion adds two figures, the dragon and the fairy, and four kinds of tiles with a symbol each: volcano, dragon, princess and magic portal. The symbol on the tile you place decides what happens.

## What is included
- 30 tiles: 6 with a volcano, 12 with a dragon, 6 with a magic portal and 6 with a princess.
- The dragon and the fairy. Both are neutral and belong to no player.
- The dragon is not in play until the first volcano tile is placed. If you draw a dragon tile before that, set it aside and draw another. When the dragon enters play, the set-aside dragon tiles are shuffled back in.
- A few tiles are special. On the tunnel tile the road is not broken. On the tile with a cloister inside a city you choose whether the follower goes in the city or in the cloister.

## Volcano
- When you place a volcano tile, the dragon is moved there at once.
- That turn you may not deploy a follower or any other figure on the tile. You may still move the fairy.
- Followers and the dragon can never share a tile.

## Dragon
- When you place a dragon tile you may first deploy a follower as usual, or move the fairy. Then the dragon moves, **before scoring**. The dragon can therefore clear a city or road that the tile has just completed.
- The dragon makes six moves in total. You start, then play goes clockwise, and each player moves the dragon one tile horizontally or vertically. If the round ends before six moves, it starts again.
- The dragon may not return to a tile it has already visited during this flight, may not enter the fairy's tile and may not enter the City of Carcassonne. If no legal move exists, the flight ends.
- Every figure on a tile the dragon enters is eaten and returns to its owner, with no points. Neutral figures such as the mage and the witch are not affected.
- When starting the game you can choose the older variant, where the dragon moves after scoring.

## Fairy
- If you do not deploy a follower, you may instead move the fairy next to one of your own followers.
- You score **1 point** at the start of your turn if the fairy is next to one of your followers.
- You score **3 points** when a feature in which your follower stands next to the fairy is scored.
- The dragon cannot enter the fairy's tile, so the figures there are safe.

## Magic portal
- When you place a portal tile you may deploy a follower either on that tile or on any tile already in play, if the feature is free and not completed.

## Princess
- When you place a princess tile that extends a city containing knights, you may choose to remove one of the knights, even your own. It returns to its owner.
- If you do, you may not deploy or move any figure that turn.
- If you choose not to remove anyone, you play as usual.

## Good to know
- The phone only shows the choices that are allowed, and the game counts the points for you.
- If the dragon eats a player's last follower in a road or city, that player's builder there is also returned. The same goes for a pig on a farm.
