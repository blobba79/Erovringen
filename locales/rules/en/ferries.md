# The Ferries

Ferries join roads that end at lakes, so that roads can cross the water. Ferries can be moved during the game.

## What is included
- 8 tiles with a lake.
- 8 ferries. They are neutral and belong to no player.

## How a turn works
- When you draw a lake tile, place it as usual and then you may deploy a follower, for example on one of its roads.
- Then you must take a ferry and place it so that it joins two of the tile's road ends into one continuous road. A road end at a lake without a ferry is closed.
- You place the ferry even if you did not deploy a follower.
- If you place a tile that extends a road containing a ferry, you may move that ferry if you wish. If the road has two ferries, you may only move the one nearest your new tile. If roads lead off in several directions, you may move the first ferry in each direction.
- Each ferry may be moved at most once per turn. The builder's double turn counts as one turn.
- If a ferry that is placed or moved joins the mage's and the witch's roads, one of them must move, just as when a tile does it.
- Roads completed by placing or moving a ferry are scored as usual.

## Scoring
- Ferries give no points of their own. Roads score as usual.
- When a ferry is moved, roads may be joined or split, and the followers on them go with them.

## Good to know
- Ferries can only lie on roads at lakes, and they cannot be eaten by the dragon or captured.
- The phone shows the ferry placements and moves that are allowed.
