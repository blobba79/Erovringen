# Crop Circles II

Six tiles with crop circles. When one is placed, all players together either get more followers on the board, or must pull them back.

## What is included
- 6 tiles with a crop circle. Each tile has a symbol: pitchfork, club or shield.
- You can play with just one tile, but it is most fun with all six.

## How a turn works
- When you place a crop circle tile, you play your whole turn as usual, including scoring.
- Then you choose, for all players, one of two options:
  - **A.** Each player may, if they wish, take a follower from their supply and place it next to one of their own followers of the same kind.
  - **B.** Each player must remove one of their own followers of that kind from the board.
- Players go in turn, starting with the player on your left, and you go last. Then the game continues with the next player's turn.
- The symbol decides the kind: pitchfork is farmers, club is thieves on roads and shield is knights in cities.
- With A, the new follower must go to a feature that already has a follower of the same kind. It does not have to be on the new tile.
- A player with no follower of that kind on the board is skipped.

## Scoring
- The crop circle itself gives no points.

## Good to know
- The abbot and neutral figures, such as the mage and the witch, are not affected.
- The phone asks you what you want to do when it is your turn to choose.
