# Inns & Cathedrals

This expansion adds inns and cathedrals that raise the score of completed roads and cities, and a big follower that weighs more in a majority.

## What is included

- 18 new tiles. Some have inns by the roads, two have cathedrals in cities.
- One big follower per player.

## How a turn works

- You place your tile as usual. Some of the tiles are special, for example with four separate city parts, or with a cloister or a crossing in the middle of a road. The game keeps track of how they count.
- When you place a follower you may choose the big follower instead of an ordinary one. It counts as one follower and is placed by the normal rules: on a road, in a city, in a field or on a cloister.
- The big follower returns after scoring like any other follower. As a farmer it stays on the field until the end of the game.

## Scoring

- **Road with an inn:** when completed it scores 2 points per tile instead of 1.
- **City with a cathedral:** when completed it scores 3 points per tile and 3 per pennant instead of 2.
- **Incomplete road with an inn:** 0 points at the end of the game.
- **Incomplete city with a cathedral:** 0 points at the end of the game, for neither tiles nor pennants.
- **Big follower:** it counts as two ordinary followers when deciding who has the most. It does not double the points. Alone in a cloister it scores the same as an ordinary follower.

## Good to know

- An inn counts for the whole road it lies beside, but not for other roads on the same tile.
- You may place inn and cathedral tiles on someone else's road or city, for example late in the game to strip a big city of its points.
- Only a few tiles have a cathedral, so building out a city with one is risky.
- If you hold several tiles you pick which one to play. If none can be placed you may swap one away.
