# Traders & Builders

The expansion gives you the builder that grants a double turn, the pig that raises farm scores, and goods that give bonus points at the end.

## What is included

- 24 new tiles. Some cities show goods: wine, grain or cloth.
- One builder and one pig per player. They are not followers.
- Trade counters: wine, grain and cloth.

## How a turn works

Instead of a follower you may place your builder or your pig on the tile you just placed.

- **The builder** may only stand on a road or in a city where you already have one of your own followers (thief or knight). How far away that follower stands does not matter, and other players' followers do not stop you. The builder does not count in a majority.
- **Double turn:** when you later place a tile that extends the road or city where your builder stands, you may take an extra turn. It is optional. First you do the whole first part (place a tile, place a piece, score), then you get a new tile and a second round. There is never a third turn.
- **The pig** may only stand on a field where you already have one of your own farmers.

The builder and the pig return to you if your last thief, knight or farmer leaves the place. The builder also returns when the road or city is scored.

## Scoring

- **Goods:** when a city with goods symbols is completed, the player who placed the last tile takes one counter for each symbol, no matter who owns the city.
- **End of the game:** the player with the most wine counters scores 10 points. The same goes for grain and cloth. In a tie, every tied player scores 10.
- **The pig:** a farmer with a pig scores 4 points instead of 3 for each completed city at the farm, but only if you have the most farmers (or share the lead) on the farm.

## Good to know

- The builder and the pig do not count when deciding who has the most.
- The bridge tile has two roads that cross without meeting. The game knows how they connect.
- If you hold several tiles you pick among them, and in a double turn you refill between the parts. If none can be placed you may swap one away.
